---
name: zoomed-out
description: Use when the user's replies turn vague, short, or noncommittal ("ok", "sure", "whatever you think") during a multi-step task — they may have lost the thread.
---

The user may have lost the thread. **Re-orient** them before continuing.

1. Pause the current work.
2. Ask where they lost the thread, offering a short numbered **map** of the conversation's turning points so they can point at one:
   - the original goal
   - each decision made since (one line each)
   - where you are now

   Mark the points most likely to have caused confusion (a jump in abstraction, an unexplained term, a silent assumption).
3. Re-explain from the point they pick, at a higher level, then confirm they're back before resuming.

Done when: the user names a point (or confirms they're fine) and you've re-explained from there.
